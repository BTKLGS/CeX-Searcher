CeX Finder

A phone-friendly starting point for finding CeX UK products, comparing prices and opening the official listing.

Important: this is an early prototype. “Search on CeX UK” opens CeX's own search. The demo listings below are fictional test data—not real products, prices or stock. Delivery availability must be confirmed on the actual CeX listing.

Results

Try a live CeX search, or tap “Try demo results” to test the filters and sorting.

Delivery filter note: the checkbox records your preference and gives you the right next step, but it does not yet automatically remove non-deliverable products from live results. A reliable live data source that exposes delivery eligibility is still needed for that.
